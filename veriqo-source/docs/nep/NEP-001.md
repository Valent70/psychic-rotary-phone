# NEP-001 — Neutral Evidence Protocol

**Status:** FROZEN — MIP-001 W0 / W5
**Constitutional basis:** Articles 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 23

---

## 1. Principle

> Same process, different permissions, visible provenance, preserved
> dissent, no automatic legal judgment.

NEP is what allows two adversarial parties to rely on one evidence
record. Its guarantee is not that VERIQO is impartial in intent — that
would be unverifiable — but that the **process is symmetric and its
asymmetries are recorded**.

---

## 2. Case constitution

Every neutral case opens with a Case Constitution binding:

```
Case · Issues · Parties · Roles · Evidence Protocol · Acquisition
Disclosure · Qualification · Confidentiality · Privilege · Challenge
Dissent · Conflict · Legal Boundary · Policy Version · Effective Time
Hash · Signature
```

Two fields carry disproportionate weight:

- **Policy Version + Effective Time** — these pin the case (Article 7).
  A later policy change cannot retroactively alter how this case was
  governed (Article 26).
- **Hash + Signature** — the constitution is itself evidence. Its terms
  cannot be quietly renegotiated mid-case.

---

## 3. Issue register (W5.1)

```
Issue → Claim → Condition → Hypothesis → Evidence
  → Contradiction → Dissent → Qualification
```

Every qualification traces back to a registered issue. A finding that
does not resolve to an issue in the register is out of scope for the
case and cannot be smuggled into the fact pack.

---

## 4. Party influence (W5.2)

Recorded for every evidence request:

```
Who requested? · Why? · What scope was proposed? · What source requested?
What source was rejected? · What priority requested? · Was it accepted?
Under what policy?
```

**Party influence never disappears from process evidence** (Article
13). Rejected requests are recorded as carefully as accepted ones —
the pattern of what a party asked for and did not get is itself
probative.

---

## 5. Dissent (W5.3)

Severity ladder: `INFORMATIONAL` → `MINOR` → `MATERIAL` → `CRITICAL`.

Critical dissent covers: integrity, provenance, source identity,
acquisition legality, methodology, proof condition, neutrality,
privilege, replay.

**Material unresolved dissent cannot silently disappear from the final
finding.** Not "should not" — the fact pack generator carries it, and a
fact pack that omits a recorded material dissent fails its own
verification.

Dissent is not an error state. A case that ends
`QUALIFIED_WITH_DISSENT` is a *better* artifact than one that reached
`QUALIFIED` by suppressing a reviewer's objection.

---

## 6. Conflict registry (W5.4)

Conflicts tracked for: people, reviewers, providers, models, vendors,
consultants, customers, affiliates, ownership, employment, contracts,
data dependencies, technical dependencies.

**Models and data dependencies are on that list deliberately.** A
reviewer's employment conflict is obvious; a shared upstream data
provider between two "independent" sources is the same class of
problem, and is easier to miss.

---

## 7. Procedural symmetry (Article 12)

Parties are treated under the same policy. Where an exception is
required — one party has a protective-order designation the other does
not, say — the exception is authorized, recorded, and visible. An
unrecorded asymmetry is a violation.

This is checkable: the ledger holds every policy decision with its
actor and purpose, so an audit can ask *"were these two parties
subject to the same rules?"* and get an answer from data rather than
from assurance.

---

## 8. The two boundaries NEP will not cross

**Article 15 — No outcome-contingent neutrality.** VERIQO may not
benefit differentially from a dispute's outcome. This is a commercial
arrangement, not a software property. `pkg/constitution` classes it
`DECLARED` and reports it as requiring external attestation — the code
surfaces the obligation and cannot discharge it.

**Article 16 — No adjudication by platform.** VERIQO produces a
qualified common evidence state. It does not determine liability. The
qualification vocabulary has no `PROVEN` state precisely so that this
boundary is enforced by the type system rather than by editorial
restraint.
